Frequently asked questions

Do I need a credit card to sign up?

No. The Free plan and the 14-day trials of Standard and Plus start without one; you add a payment method only when you keep a paid plan.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes. Standard and Plus come with a 14-day trial with every feature of the plan. At the end of the trial you choose a plan or stay on Free.

What if I decide to cancel my plan?

Your workspace moves to the Free plan at the end of the billing period. Your projects stay, and you can export them at any time.

Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?

Yes, from the billing settings. Switching to annual billing takes effect immediately and credits the rest of your current month.
